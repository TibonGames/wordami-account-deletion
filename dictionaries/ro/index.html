Romanian word list used in Wordami

Wordami (a word game by Merry Moth Games) uses a modified version of the Romanian Hunspell dictionary ro_RO 3.3.10 (rospell) by the Rospell Team – Lucian Constantin and contributors. The original and the modified list are available under the Mozilla Public License 1.1.

Files

ro_ROEdited.zip – modified word list (1 541 045 words, UTF-8, one word per line), 3.7 MB ATTRIBUTION.txt – source, authors and the list of modifications LICENSE.txt – Mozilla Public License 1.1

Modifications

  • All word forms generated from ro_RO.dic and the affix rules in ro_RO.aff.
  • Letters ş/ţ (cedilla) unified to ș/ț (comma below).
  • Removed: proper nouns, abbreviations, hyphenated forms, faulty forms and offensive vocabulary.
  • In the game the list is converted into a binary search structure (trie).

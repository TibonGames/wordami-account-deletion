Ukrainian word list used in Wordami

Wordami (a word game by Merry Moth Games) uses a modified version of the Ukrainian Hunspell dictionary uk_UA 6.7.5 (dict_uk / VESUM) by Andriy Rysin, Vasyl Starko, the BrUK team and the spell-uk contributors. The original and the modified list are available under the Mozilla Public License 1.1.

Files

uk_UAEdited.zip – modified word list (3 148 084 words, UTF-8, one word per line), 9.5 MB ATTRIBUTION.txt – source, authors and the list of modifications LICENSE.txt – Mozilla Public License 1.1

Modifications

  • All word forms generated from uk_UA.dic and the affix rules in uk_UA.aff.
  • Removed: proper nouns, abbreviations, hyphenated forms and offensive vocabulary.
  • Apostrophes removed from words (п'ять → пять).
  • In the game the list is converted into a binary search structure (trie).

Original source

  • LibreOffice/dictionaries – uk_UA (files downloaded 2026-11-25)
  • brown-uk/dict_uk v6.7.5 – Hunspell export

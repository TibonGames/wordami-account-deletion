2. Jakie dane przetwarzamy.

  • Konto i logowanie: identyfikator gracza Google Play Games i powiązany z nim identyfikator konta PlayFab. Logowanie odbywa się przez Gry Play; nie otrzymujemy Twojego hasła ani adresu e-mail.

  • Dane techniczne: informacje o urządzeniu i czas aktywności wysyłane przez usługę PlayFab.
  • Reklamy: gra wyświetla reklamy Google AdMob. Google może przetwarzać m.in. identyfikator reklamowy, dane o urządzeniu, adres IP i interakcje z reklamami – na własnych zasadach (policies.google.com/privacy). Nie powiązujemy tych danych z Twoim kontem w grze. W Europejskim Obszarze Gospodarczym, Wielkiej Brytanii i Szwajcarii przy pierwszym uruchomieniu pokazujemy komunikat o zgodzie na reklamy (Google User Messaging Platform); reklamy ładujemy dopiero po jego obsłużeniu. Zgodę możesz w każdej chwili zmienić lub wycofać, równie łatwo jak ją wyraziłeś(-aś): w Ustawieniach gry, przyciskiem „Ustawienia prywatności (reklamy)” (widocznym w regionach, w których obowiązuje komunikat o zgodzie lub o prywatności, np. w EOG, Wielkiej Brytanii, Szwajcarii i wybranych stanach USA). Personalizację reklam możesz też ograniczyć w ustawieniach reklam swojego urządzenia z Androidem lub konta Google. Jeśli nie wyrazisz zgody, nadal możesz widzieć reklamy, ale nie będą one personalizowane i mogą być wyświetlane bez użycia identyfikatorów reklamowych (reklamy ograniczone).
  • Na urządzeniu: ustawienia, zapis postępu i listy znalezionych słów (do usunięcia przez odinstalowanie gry lub funkcję usunięcia konta).

Nie zbieramy: lokalizacji, kontaktów, zdjęć, mikrofonu, kamery, danych płatniczych. Gra nie ma zakupów w aplikacji ani czatu.

Privacy Policy – Wordami

Effective: 20 November 2026

1. Controller. Dominik Stachura, trading as Merry Moth Games, al. Jana Pawła II 57/232, 31-864 Kraków, Poland; email: contact.merrymothgames@gmail.com; phone: +48 690 203 214.

2. Data we process.

  • Account and sign-in: your Google Play Games player ID and the linked PlayFab account ID. Sign-in goes through Play Games; we do not receive your password or email address.
  • Game profile: nickname, level, title, avatar, coins, power-ups, progress and login streaks. Nickname, level, title and avatar are visible to other players (e.g. in search, friends list and matches). Do not put personal data in your nickname.
  • Gameplay activity: scores and words from daily mode and online matches, leaderboard, friends, friend and match invitations.
  • Technical data: device information and active time sent by the PlayFab service.
  • Ads: the game shows Google AdMob ads. Google may process e.g. the advertising ID, device data, IP address and ad interactions under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not link this data to your game account. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we show a consent message for ads at first launch (Google User Messaging Platform); ads are loaded only after it has been handled. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: in the game's Settings, using the "Privacy settings (ads)" button (shown in regions where a consent or privacy message applies, e.g. the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and selected US states). You can also limit ad personalisation in the ad settings of your Android device or Google Account. If you do not give consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised and may be shown without the use of advertising identifiers (limited ads).
  • On your device: settings, saved progress and found-word lists (removed by uninstalling the game or by deleting your account).

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or payment data. The game has no in-app purchases and no chat.

3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR). Providing the game and online features – Art. 6(1)(b); security and abuse prevention – (f); personalised ads and advertising identifiers – consent, (a) (you give consent in Google's message in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and you can change or withdraw it at any time).

4. Recipients. Microsoft (PlayFab – hosting of account and match data), Google (Play Games, AdMob). The provider (Microsoft) may store and process account and match data in the United States or in other countries where it or its subcontractors operate, including outside the European Economic Area. Transfers outside the EEA are made on the basis of the transfer mechanisms provided for in Chapter V of the GDPR, identified in the provider's data protection terms (PlayFab Privacy Terms). On request we will tell you which mechanism applies to you.

5. Retention. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Technical data linked to the account (events and device information collected by PlayFab) is deleted together with the account: after deletion the data is removed first from the provider's core system and then from its ancillary systems, within 30 days. Data held by Google follows Google's policies.

6. Deleting your account and data. In the game: Settings → Delete account. Without the game: account deletion page (https://tibongames.github.io/wordami-account-deletion/). We delete the PlayFab account, profile, statistics, leaderboard entries, friends, invitations and matches, within 30 days at most. We cannot delete data held by Google (Play Games profile, advertising ID) – manage it in your Google/Android settings. Local data on your other devices is removed by uninstalling the game. After your account is deleted we keep, for 30 days only, a deletion confirmation containing the technical identifier of the deleted account (no nickname, email or gameplay data).

7. Your rights. Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: contact.merrymothgames@gmail.com.

8. Users in the USA and other regions. If you live in a US state whose law grants privacy rights (e.g. California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas), you may have the right to: know what data we process about you, have it deleted and corrected, and opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising. You can make your advertising choice in the privacy message shown in your region and in the game's Settings (the "Privacy settings (ads)" button). You can delete your account and data as described in section 6. Send any other requests to the address in section 1; we will respond within the time limits set by applicable law. We do not discriminate against people who exercise these rights. Users in other regions may have rights under local laws; write to us and we will consider your request.

9. Children. The game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 16 years of age. If you believe a child has created an account, write to the address in section 1 and we will delete the data.

10. Changes. We will post changes on this page with a new date.
